# Versus Mode

**The Geometry Dash competition hub.**

Bring your friends together, check your Globed room, choose a challenge, and compete across classic and custom Versus formats.

## Features in the 1.0 launch line

- Geometry Dash-inspired lobby with a Level Complete-style presentation.
- Globed connection and room status.
- Private Versus room flow that reuses Globed rooms.
- Race, Survival, Dual, and Platformer mode selection UI.
- Rank ladder and player profile preview.
- Globed-powered multiplayer foundation and voice support where Globed provides it.

**Requirements:** Geometry Dash 2.2081, Geode SDK 5.11.2, and Globed 2.3.0 beta or newer.

Some online features remain in development until Globed-native matchmaking, validated session handoff, and synchronized match results are implemented and tested.
