# Matchmaking protocol notes

## State machine

`not_found -> queued -> ready-check -> ready`

A queued ticket expires after 90 seconds. A ready-check expires after 120 seconds. Leaving or cancelling removes a queued ticket and cancels a not-yet-ready match.

## Matching rules

- One shared global queue, independent of current Globed room.
- Exact game-mode match.
- Casual queue only in this prototype.
- Region is retained as metadata but does not constrain pairing; latency-aware regional pools are a later improvement.
- A pair must complete a two-player ready check.

## Trust boundaries

The client cannot choose its opponent or match ID. Tickets are generated by the service. The client cannot submit wins or rank changes. The service does not expose ranked queue until verified account identity and server-authoritative gameplay results exist.

## Not yet implemented

The service does not create Globed rooms, transfer players, synchronize gameplay, or persist player profiles. A ready match is not yet a playable match.
